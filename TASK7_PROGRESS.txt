TASK 7 — EXPERIMENTAL AFFINITY ANALYSIS
Completed: 2026-09-04

GOAL
Sort experimental affinity data from lowest to highest Kd and calculate
Spearman rank correlations between experimental affinity and:
1. standalone Boltz-2 affinity predictions
2. AF3 + Boltz-2 affinity-module predictions

INTERPRETATION
Lower experimental Kd corresponds to stronger binding.
Rank 1 therefore represents the strongest experimentally measured binder.

PRIMARY ANALYSIS SET
The same 8 held-out complexes from Task 6 were used for the primary
head-to-head comparison. These cases were not used for Task 6 training
or model selection.

SUPPLEMENTAL FULL STANDALONE BOLTZ ANALYSIS
Usable Task 5 cases: N = 301
Spearman rho = -0.264431

The full-set Boltz result is supplemental and is not directly compared
to the AF3 held-out coefficient because the evaluated sample sets differ.

INTERPRETATION
On the matched held-out set, standalone Boltz-2 showed weak negative
agreement with the experimentally measured affinity ranking.

The AF3 + pretrained/calibrated Boltz affinity Head-1 showed strong
positive agreement with the experimental ranking (rho = 0.8095).

Fine-tuning the final Head-1 regression layer did not improve
generalization and instead produced a negative held-out rank
correlation, consistent with the Task 6 conclusion that fine-tuning
overfit the small pilot dataset.
