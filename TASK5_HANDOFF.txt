RECOVERY APPROACH
-----------------
83 additional records were recovered from aptamers that were not in
the original direct-ready set but had canonical DNA/RNA backbones and
validated ACCEPT targets.

These were modeled using an unmodified canonical DNA/RNA backbone.

This is a modeling approximation.

Chemical modifications present in experimental aptamers are not
explicitly represented in the recovery predictions.

The resulting Boltz-2 affinity values SHOULD NOT be interpreted as
experimentally validated or quantitatively reliable aptamer binding
affinities.

No model retraining was performed.
