IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION / LIMITATION
-------------------------------------
Boltz-2 affinity modeling was developed for protein-small-molecule
complexes.

Task 4 modified the Boltz-2 workflow so RNA/DNA aptamer chains could be
designated as affinity binders.

Therefore, the Task 5 affinity values demonstrate successful technical
execution of the adapted Boltz-2 workflow on aptamer complexes.

They SHOULD NOT be interpreted as experimentally validated or
quantitatively reliable aptamer binding affinities.

Some protein targets are also represented by full-length UniProt
sequences when the literature target refers to a domain, peptide, or
other biological construct. This is another limitation of the current
automated target-resolution workflow.

No model retraining was performed.
